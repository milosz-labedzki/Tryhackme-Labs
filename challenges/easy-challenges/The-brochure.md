### Tools Used

- Browser
- Instagram (or public Instagram viewers like imginn / greatfon)
- CyberChef

### Step-by-Step Methodology

- **Step 1** - Download the task file and open the provided image (Byte Lotus brochure).
- **Step 2** - Analyze the text on the image. Notice the mentions of Instagram and “Concierge VERA”.
- **Step 3** - Search Instagram for the hotel-related account (Byte Lotus Resort / Byte Lotus Hotel).
- **Step 4** - Check who the hotel account is following. It follows only one account: `@veratheconcierge`.
- **Step 5** - Open Vera’s profile. There are three posts containing parts of a Base64 string.
- **Step 6** - Copy the Base64 fragments from all three posts and combine them into one complete string.
- **Step 7** - Decode the full Base64 string using CyberChef (From Base64) to obtain the flag.
