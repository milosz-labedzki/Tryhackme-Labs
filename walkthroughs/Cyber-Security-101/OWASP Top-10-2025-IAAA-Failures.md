* `IAAA` - Identification, Authentication, Authorization, and Accountability; a foundational access control framework defining the four sequential stages required to manage access and track user activities within a system.

* `Identity` - The unique set of claims or identifiers (such as a username or account ID) presented by an entity to state who or what it is within a system.

* `Authentication` - The security process of verifying and validating the claimed identity of a user, device, or system using credentials (e.g., passwords, biometrics, tokens).

* `Authorisation` - The process of granting or denying specific permissions, privileges, and access rights to an authenticated entity based on system security policies.

* `Accountability` - The security principle of ensuring that all system actions and activities can be uniquely traced back to the responsible entity through logging and auditing.
