* `Cipher` - An algorithm or predefined set of mathematical steps used to perform encryption and decryption on data.

* `Key` - A secret piece of information or bit sequence used by a cipher to encrypt plaintext or decrypt ciphertext.

* `Encryption` - The cryptographic process of transforming readable plaintext into unreadable ciphertext to protect data confidentiality.

* `Decryption` - The cryptographic process of converting scrambled ciphertext back into its original, readable plaintext form using a secret key.

* `XOR operation` - Exclusive OR; a bitwise logical operation that outputs true (1) only when input bits differ, widely used in cryptography due to its self-inverse property.

* `Modulo Operation` - A mathematical operation that calculates the remainder after integer division, serving as a foundational concept in modular arithmetic and asymmetric cryptography.
