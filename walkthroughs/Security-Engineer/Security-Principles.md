- `CIA Triad` - Core security model focusing on Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability of data.


- `DAD Triad` - The threat counterpart to CIA, representing Disclosure, Alteration, and Destruction or Denial of services.


- `Bell-LaPadula Model` - Security model enforcing confidentiality with No Read Up and No Write Down rules to prevent unauthorized data leaks.


- `Biba Model` - Security model focused on data integrity with No Read Down and No Write Up rules to avoid importing untrusted data.


- `Clark-Wilson Model` - Business and banking integrity model enforcing well-defined transactional procedures and separation of duties.


- `ISO/IEC` - International standards and frameworks providing guidelines for information security management best practices.


- `Trust but Verify` - Traditional security approach where entities are implicitly trusted within a network after initial verification.


- `Zero Trust` - Modern security model based on "Never trust, always verify," requiring continuous authentication and authorization for every access request.


- `Vulnerability` - A weakness, flaw, or misconfiguration in a system or software that can be exploited by an attacker.


- `Threat` - A potential danger, malicious actor, or event that could exploit a vulnerability to compromise a system.


- `Risk` - The probability and business impact resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability; it cannot be completely eliminated, only mitigated to an acceptable level based on cost-efficiency.
