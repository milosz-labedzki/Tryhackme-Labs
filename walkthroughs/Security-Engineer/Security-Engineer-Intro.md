* `Security Engineer` - An IT professional responsible for designing, building, and maintaining an organization's security infrastructure, such as firewalls and SIEM systems.


* `Asset Management/Asset Inventory` - The practice of tracking all hardware, software, and data assets to prevent forgotten devices and unpatched applications from becoming attack vectors.


* `Change Management` - A structured process for evaluating, approving, and recording system modifications to avoid outages, untracked vulnerabilities, and unverified configurations.


* `Ensuring Awareness` - Educating employees on security risks like phishing emails and strong password practices to prevent human vulnerability from being exploited to gain network access.


* `Managing Risks` - The process of identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing security risks to balance protection costs with asset values instead of aiming for impossible 100% security.
