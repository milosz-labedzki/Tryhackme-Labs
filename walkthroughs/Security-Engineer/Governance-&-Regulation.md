* `Strategy` - High-level security plan that aligns security investments and goals with overall business objectives.


* `Policies and procedures` - Rules defining security requirements paired with step-by-step instructions to execute them consistently.


* `Risk management` - Process of identifying and handling security risks according to business tolerance, acknowledging 100% security is impossible.


* `Performance measurement` - Tracking metrics and statistics to evaluate the effectiveness of security controls and incident responses.


* `Compliance` - Ensuring total adherence to laws, industry standards, and regulatory requirements.

* `GRC` - Framework integrating governance, risk management, and compliance so that organizational security processes work cohesively together.


* `General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)` - European Union regulation protecting personal data privacy, requiring user consent and penalizing companies for breaches.


* `Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)` - Security standard required for any merchant or store processing credit card payments.


* `NIST 800-53` - Comprehensive catalog of security and privacy controls for organizational systems.


* `NIST 800-63B` - Specific standard focusing on digital identity guidelines, authentication, and password management.


* `ISO/IEC 27001` - International standard requiring a living Information Security Management System (ISMS) that continuously updates and improves.
