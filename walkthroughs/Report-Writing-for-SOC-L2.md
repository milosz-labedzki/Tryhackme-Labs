* `Responsibilities of Level 2` - L2 does full attack analysis and builds a clear timeline. They cannot just copy raw logs because managers and clients will not understand them.

* `Communication Channels` - Use internal chat for team talk, official emails for customers, and secure encrypted tools when sending passwords.

* `Reporting to Company Leadership` - Explain what happened in simple terms. Show the business impact, what the SOC team did to stop it, and how to prevent it next time.

* `Contacting MSSP Customers` - Explain the incident in simple terms to reassure the client, list what the SOC already did, and clearly highlight the action steps the client needs to take.


* `DFIR Handover Notes` - Create a detailed step-by-step timeline with exact timestamps and include all gathered facts so the DFIR team can continue the investigation without starting over.


* `AI in SOC Report Writing` - Use AI to organize and format raw data into clean reports, but always verify the output for mistakes and avoid using AI to make direct changes on live production systems.
