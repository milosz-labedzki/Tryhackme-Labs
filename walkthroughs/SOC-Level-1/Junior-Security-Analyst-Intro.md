* `Security Analyst` - A cybersecurity professional responsible for monitoring, protecting, and defending an organization's networks, infrastructure, and digital assets against cyber threats and security incidents.

* `SOC and Your Team` - The operational framework and collaborative workforce within a Security Operations Center, comprising Tier 1–3 analysts, threat hunters, incident responders, and managers working together to maintain continuous security posture.

* `Your Daily Duties` - Core operational responsibilities executed by a security analyst, including real-time alert triage, log monitoring, incident investigation, vulnerability assessment, threat intelligence parsing, and security reporting.
