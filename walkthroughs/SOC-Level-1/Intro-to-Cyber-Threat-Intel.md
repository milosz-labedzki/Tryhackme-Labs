* `TLP:CLEAR` - Information has no restrictions on disclosure; it can be posted publicly or shared without limit.

* `TLP:GREEN` - Information sharing is limited to a specific community or organization; cannot be shared publicly.


* `TLP:AMBER` - Information sharing is restricted to the organization's employees and trusted third parties on a need-to-know basis.


* `TLP:RED` - Information is highly sensitive and restricted strictly to specific participants in a meeting or direct recipients.


* `TLP:AMBER+STRICT` - Restricts information strictly to the recipient's organization only (prevents sharing with third-party clients/contractors).


* `STIX (Structured Threat Information eXpression)` - A standardized, machine-readable language and serialization format (JSON) used to structure cyber threat intelligence data.


* `TAXII (Trusted Automated eXchange of Intelligence Information)` - An application layer protocol designed to exchange threat intelligence (STIX) securely via HTTPS APIs.
