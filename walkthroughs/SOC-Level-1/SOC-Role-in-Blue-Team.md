* `Security Operations Center (SOC)` - A centralized facility within an organization responsible for continuously monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to security incidents across the enterprise IT infrastructure.

* `Security Hierarchy` - The organizational structure dividing security responsibilities across operational levels, spanning Executives (business objectives), Security Leadership (strategic programs), Security Managers (team management), and Technical staff (hands-on execution).

* `Red Team` - An offensive security group that simulates real-world adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) to evaluate and challenge the effectiveness of an organization's defensive posture.

* `GRC Team` - Governance, Risk, and Compliance team; responsible for aligning IT security with business goals, managing organizational risk, and ensuring compliance with industry regulations and standards.

* `Blue Team` - A defensive security group responsible for protecting organizational assets, continuously monitoring systems for suspicious activity, and responding to cyber threats in real time.

* `Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT)` - A specialized unit of security experts dedicated to investigating, containing, eradicating, and recovering from active cybersecurity breaches and high-priority incidents.
