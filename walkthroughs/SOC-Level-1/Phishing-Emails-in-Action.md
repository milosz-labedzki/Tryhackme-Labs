* `Spoofed email address` - An email sender address forged by an attacker to make a message look like it came from a trusted person or legitimate domain.

* `URL shortening` - Using online redirection services to turn long links into short URLs, often hiding the real, malicious destination from the victim.

* `Branded HTML` - Email design that copies logos, colors, and layouts of known companies to make a fake email look official and trustworthy.

* `Pixel tracking` - Embedding a tiny, invisible 1x1 image in an HTML email to notify the attacker when and where the email was opened.

* `Link manipulation` - Technical tricks used to disguise malicious web addresses, such as displaying a safe URL text while linking to a harmful site.

* `Hyperlink Tracking` - Redirecting email links through tracking servers to log when a victim clicks a link and collect details about their device.

* `Credential harvesting` - Using fake login portals to trick users into typing in sensitive account credentials like usernames and passwords.

* `BCC` - Blind Carbon Copy; a hidden email field used by attackers to send mass phishing emails to many victims at once without showing the recipient list.

* `Poor grammar and typos` - Frequent spelling mistakes and odd phrasing in phishing emails, often caused by translation tools or quick creation.

* `Attachments` - Files sent along with an email (such as malicious PDFs, Office docs, or ZIP files) that install malware when opened by the target.
