* `Alert Reporting` - The process of documenting security alert investigation findings, evidence, context, and mitigation steps into formal ticketing systems or incident management platforms.

* `Alert Escalation` - The operational action of forwarding a security event to higher-tier analysts, CIRT, or management when an alert exceeds Tier 1 scope, requires advanced analysis, or indicates a confirmed threat.

* `Communication` - The clear, timely exchange of technical facts, risk assessments, and incident updates among analysts, response teams, management, and key stakeholders.

* `Report Format (5Ws)` - An incident documentation framework that structures analysis by defining Who (accounts/threat actors), What (event type), When (timestamps), Where (affected systems/IPs), and Why/How (attack vectors and root causes).

* `Escalation Steps` - The standardized, sequential protocol that defines how and when an alert transitions from initial triage to Tier 2/3 review, management notification, and active incident response invocation.
