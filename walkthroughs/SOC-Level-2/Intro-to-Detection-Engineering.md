* `Detection Engineering` - Proactive creation and tuning of queries, rules, and log filters that allow the SOC to detect and investigate threats effectively.


* `The Detection Gap` - The space between total possible malicious activity and what your tools actually catch; aiming to detect absolute 100% of things is impractical due to false positives and noise.


* `Attack Chain` - A sequence of techniques executed by an attacker rather than a single isolated action, which gives defenders multiple points along the kill chain to spot them.


* `Precision and Recall` - Striking the right balance in detection rules to avoid getting flooded with false alarms while still catching genuine attacks.
