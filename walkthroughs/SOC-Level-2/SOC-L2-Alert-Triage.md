* `SOC Level 2 Workflow` - Advanced incident handling focusing on deep-dive investigation, alert escalation, complex log correlation, and strategic threat containment when standard playbooks are insufficient.

* `L1 vs L2 Alert Triage` - Tier 1 performs initial alert triage and filtering, whereas Tier 2 conducts root-cause analysis, incident scope determination, and active threat mitigation.

* `Log Analysis as L2` - In-depth analysis and correlation of multi-source telemetry (EDR, SIEM, firewall, auth logs) to reconstruct attacker actions and discover subtle anomalies.

* `Build Detailed Timelines` - Reconstructing the chronological order of adversary activities across impacted systems to map out initial access, lateral movement, and full incident impact.

* `Threat Hunting Loop` - Proactive, hypothesis-driven iteration of searching through enterprise logs to uncover hidden threats that bypassed automated security alerts.

* `Threat Response` - The execution of tactical actions to mitigate damage, eliminate attacker access, and restore operational state following incident findings.

* `Response to Major Incidents` - Prioritizing immediate threat containment (e.g., halting active exfiltration) over full investigation completion under high-pressure scenarios.

* `Containment` - Restricting adversary activity and preventing incident spread by isolating hosts, blocking malicious network traffic, or disabling compromised user accounts.

* `Eradication` - Completely removing threat components from the environment by deleting malware, revoking rogue permissions, and rotating compromised credentials.

* `Recovery` - Safely bringing impacted systems back into production, removing containment restrictions, and applying heightened monitoring to prevent reinfection.
