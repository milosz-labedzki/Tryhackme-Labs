* `Detection` - Proactive hunting for threats and anomalies across systems, since users are often unaware of stealthy attacks or phishing emails.


* `Analysis` - Investigating an incident to determine what happened, verify true positives, and track attacker progression through the Cyber Kill Chain.


* `Common IR Triggers` - Security events that signal an active incident and require immediate response, such as impossible travel logins, brute-force attacks (Windows Event ID 4625 followed by successful 4624), or sophisticated phishing emails.
