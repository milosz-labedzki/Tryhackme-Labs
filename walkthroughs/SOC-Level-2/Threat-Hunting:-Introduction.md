* `Threat Hunting` - Proactive and manual search through logs and networks to find hidden attackers that bypass automated security tools.


* `Threat Hunting vs Incident Response` - Incident Response is reactive (triggered by alerts), while Threat Hunting is proactive (assumes the attacker is already inside without any alerts).


* `Dwell Time` - The total duration an attacker remains undetected inside a compromised network, from initial intrusion to actual detection.


* `Hypothesis-Driven Hunting` - Proactive threat hunting based on specific assumptions about attacker behaviors, TTPs, or intelligence reports.


* `Intelligence-Driven Hunting` - Threat hunting initiated and guided by external Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) feeds, reports, and indicators.


* `Indicator-Driven Hunting` - Searching for specific traces and static digital evidence (like hashes or IPs) left behind by attackers.


* `Attack Signatures` - Patterns or rules used to detect known attack behaviors and techniques, effective even if the attacker renames files or changes hashes.


* `Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)` - Physical and digital evidence of an ongoing or past security breach, such as malicious file hashes, C2 IP addresses, or suspicious file paths.


* `Behavioral Pattern Analysis` - Examining sequences of user and system activity to identify malicious behavior rather than relying on static indicators.


* `Logical Queries and Anomaly Detection` - Using structured search queries in SIEM systems to filter baseline traffic and spot unusual activities that deviate from normal patterns.
