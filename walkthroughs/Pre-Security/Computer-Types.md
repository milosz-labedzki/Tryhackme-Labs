* `Desktop` - A personal computer designed to be used in one place, usually on a desk.


* `Workstation` - A powerful computer designed for demanding professional tasks, such as 3D rendering or video editing.


* `Server` - A computer that provides services, data, and resources to other devices over a network.


* `Laptop` - A portable computer that allows users to work in different places.


* `Smartphone` - A small handheld computer that runs applications, connects to networks, and performs many different tasks.


* `Tablet` - A portable touchscreen computer with a larger display than most smartphones.


* `IoT device` - A physical device connected to a network that can communicate and exchange data with other devices or systems.


* `Embedded Computer` - A computer built into a larger device to perform specific tasks, such as controlling a washing machine or a car's ABS system.
