* `CIA Triad` - A foundational information security framework centered on three core principles—Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability—designed to guide security policies and safeguard data.

* `Confidentiality` - The security principle ensuring that sensitive data and resources are accessible only to authorized individuals or processes, preventing unauthorized disclosure.

* `Integrity` - The security principle ensuring that data remains accurate, complete, and trustworthy by preventing unauthorized or accidental modification, tampering, or deletion.

* `Availability` - The security principle ensuring that systems, applications, and data remain operational, reachable, and accessible to authorized users whenever needed.
